# Scribble

Manage your personal projects with tools for ideas, goals and deep branch organization.
No matter if you're a software developer seeking for a way to keep track of your roadmap and update ideas, or a student who needs to organize his next presentation - Scribble is the right thing for you.

## What it does

Create a new project. You can fill it with notes to keep track of ideas and goals that mark achievements on the road to accomplishing the project. Create a deeper structure using branches, which represent major parts or milestones of a project. Add notes, goals and more branches to them. Scribble is a unique way to collect all your ideas and accomplishments - focused on a easy-to-use interface so you don't have to read a manual to get started.

## Perspective

This is just the beginning. Scribble in its form now only marks the start of something bigger. The roadmap for Scribble is full of plans for deeper structuring and more detailed management. After a few months, after Scribble 1 has proven its funtionality and features have been added, it will be time for Scribble AI. More information about Scribble AI will soon be available in this repository.
