# Privacy Policy

Scribble respects your privacy.

## Data Collection

Scribble does not collect, transmit, or share any data. All data you enter into the app (such as devices and charging values) is stored locally on your device.

## Data Usage

The app uses your locally stored data exclusively to provide its functionality.

## Data Sharing

Scribble does not share data with third parties. No analytics, tracking, or advertising services are used.

## Data Storage

All data remains on your device and is not accessible to the developer as well as any other third party.
